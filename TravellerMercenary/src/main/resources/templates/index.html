About this preview

An original interface for Classic Traveller Book 4 military careers, with Book 1 enlistment, ageing, and benefits. Recruiting mercenary units, contracts, equipment purchasing, and battle resolution are later stages.

The printed general-assignment text says INT 8+, while its table says EDU 8+. This preview follows the table: EDU 8+. The selected service-world TL controls MOS modifiers and available equipment specialties. Tech levels 5–15 are supported.

Decisions and dice are resolved on the server. Past stages remain readable, with no changing a completed posting. Save files include the entire in-progress career. This local preview has a shared saved-record library and no accounts.
