        Rules and worksheet conventions

        Uses a selection of components and programs from the 1986 Book 8 construction rules. Equipment with a listed mass and no listed volume is allocated 1 L per kg. This worksheet fits installed gear inside the chassis or head and does not assign gear to individual arms.

        For battery-only ground drives, enter a transmission rating. Other battery-only designs report endurance from stored energy and the design’s draw. Weapon-law messages are prompts for the referee.
