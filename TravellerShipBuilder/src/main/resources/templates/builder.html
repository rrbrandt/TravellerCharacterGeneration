01

Hull & identity

Standard hulls: 100, 200, 400, 600, 800 and 1,000 tons. Custom hulls may be 100–5,000 tons; drive performance uses the next listed hull size.

The discount is for an existing design plan, not merely a standard hull. New custom designs include the 1% architect's fee.

02

Engineering & fuel

Plant letter must be at least as high as both drives. A dash in the Book 2 performance table means that drive cannot be used in that hull. Computer jump support includes the bis models.

03

Accommodation & crew

Four tons per stateroom, half a ton per low berth. Emergency berths hold four people but are not passenger accommodation. A commercial crew needs individual staterooms.

04

Hardpoints & armament

One hardpoint per 100 tons; one turret per hardpoint. Each installed turret uses one ton for fire control. Weapon counts are totals across all mounts.
