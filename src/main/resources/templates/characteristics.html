ROLL REFERENCE / VERSION 1.0

What your rolls mean

Each characteristic starts with 2D6: 2–12, with an average of 7. Higher scores indicate more of that characteristic.

CharacteristicScoreMeaning
STR · Strength—Physical power and muscular force.
DEX · Dexterity—Coordination, agility and fine control.
END · Endurance—Stamina, resilience and staying power.
INT · Intelligence—Reasoning, understanding and problem solving.
EDU · Education—Accumulated knowledge and learning.
SOC · Social standing—Position in society and social influence.
Reading the numbers
Score / UPPGeneral reading
1–2Very low
3–5Below average
6–8Typical range
9–10 / 9–AAbove average
11–12 / B–CExceptional
13–15 / D–FBeyond an initial 2D6 roll; possible through later increases

These descriptions are roleplaying guidance, not extra bonuses or fixed qualifications. Education is distinct from intelligence; Social Standing is distinct from cash or military rank.

UPP order: STR DEX END INT EDU SOC. A=10, B=11, C=12, D=13, E=14, F=15.
