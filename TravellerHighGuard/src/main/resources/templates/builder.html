01
LAY THE KEEL

Hull & mission

Configurations 1, 2 & 6 are streamlined; 3–5 permit fuel skimming. Dispersed structures and planetoids remain in space.

02
RANGE, THRUST & POWER

Engineering

How engineering is calculated

Maneuver drives take (3M − 1)% of the hull; jump drives take (J + 1)%. Plant size depends on TL and plant number. Output is 1% of hull tonnage × plant number in energy points. Equipment consumes EP first; remaining EP determines agility, capped by maneuver rating.

The plant number must meet both jump and maneuver ratings. Higher numbers can supply weapons and screens.

03
STAY IN THE FIGHT

Armor & screens

Black globes require TL15 and approval. They absorb energy into capacitors; they are not ordinary commercial equipment.

04
BUILD YOUR BATTERIES

Weapons

One spinal mount. Each bay is a battery and reserves ten potential hardpoints; turret batteries group 1–10 identical mounts. Set unused groups to zero mounts.

Battery rules & tech bonuses

Lasers, missiles and sandcasters allow three weapons per turret; energy guns allow two; particle weapons allow one. Every battery of a weapon category must have the same USP factor. Bays and turrets cannot share the same category, and a spinal weapon excludes secondary weapons of its category.

Higher-tech components improve some turret factors. Bay entries use the latest available component at or below the ship's TL, keeping an earlier component when a later table row is blank.

05
PEOPLE MAKE THE SHIP

Crew & quarters

Required crew is calculated by section. Quarters include single rooms for section heads; passengers always get individual staterooms. Frozen watch needs ordinary low berths, in addition to low passengers.

06
THE SHIP WITHIN THE SHIP

Carried craft & cargo

Enter a carried craft's own displacement, purchase price and crew. These are carried references; this release does not design their internal systems.

This is Classic Traveller Book 5: High Guard (1980), with its own construction calculations and saved ships. Book 2 remains available with its lettered drives and original designs. Imported ships are copies with a conversion record and an untouched source snapshot.

Construction preview: 100–1,000,000-ton starships and non-starships, TL7–15. Fleet combat, naval careers and internal small-craft design are separate future stages. Carried craft are listed references; the final fighter-squadron USP position stays 0 until squadron organization is implemented.

Uniform turret groups are supported. Mixed weapons within an individual turret are not represented; import flags regrouping for review. Automated crew allocation rounds required personnel upward and uses one individual room per occupied section head plus a troop commander. Extra mission fittings have no assumed combat effects. A return conversion to Book 2 is not included in this first release.
