    Reading your survey

    The world profile

    A 7 8 8 8 9 9 − C

    Starport · size · atmosphere · hydrographics · population · government · law − technology. Codes above 9 use letters. For high rolled values, extended hex skips I and O.

    Trade shorthand

    Ag Agricultural · Na Non-agricultural · In Industrial · Ni Non-industrial · Ri Rich · Po Poor. These are the basic Classic trade classifications.

    Rules & referee judgement

    1981 hydrographics uses atmosphere, not size; size 0 forces atmosphere and hydrographics to 0. A low population does not silently rewrite government or technology. Unusual results invite a story.

    What this survey covers

    Mainworlds, starports, bases, gas giants, UWP explanations and dice history. Travel zones and jump routes require referee decisions. Encounters, equipment and expanded Book 6 star systems are outside this preview.
